Rachel Thompson works as a patient advocate helping injury victims navigate the medical system during recovery. She helps people understand their treatment options, medical billing, and how to document injuries effectively for both health and legal purposes. At Injury Victim Guide, she covers medical documentation, navigating healthcare after an injury, and the intersection of medical treatment and legal claims.
