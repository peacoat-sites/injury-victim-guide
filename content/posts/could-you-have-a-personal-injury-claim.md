After an accident or injury, it is hard to know whether what happened to you is just bad luck or something that might actually support a legal claim. The questions swirl fast: Was someone else at fault? Did I wait too long? Is it even worth looking into?

This quick 7-question quiz is a gentle starting point for that reflection. It does not evaluate your case or predict any outcome. It simply helps you think through the common factors people consider before talking to a licensed attorney, so you can decide whether a conversation is worth having.
