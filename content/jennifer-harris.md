Jennifer Harris is a legal researcher who spent years working alongside personal injury attorneys before turning that knowledge into consumer-facing content. She is not an attorney and does not provide legal advice, but she understands the claims process in depth and writes to help injury victims understand what they are dealing with before their first consultation. At Injury Victim Guide, she covers the claims process, legal terminology, and how to document and protect a case from day one.
